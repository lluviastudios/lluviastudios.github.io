**Effective date:** 27 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lluvia Studios ("we", "us") and the third-party services built into our mobile games handle information when you play them. It applies to every game that links to this page, currently:

- **Crowd Merge** (Android, `com.lluviastudios.crowdmerge`)

## 1. Who we are

The data controller is **Lluvia Studios**, based in Spain. Contact: **lluviastudios@outlook.com**.

We do not run our own servers for our games. Most of the processing described below is carried out by the service providers listed in section 5.

## 2. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, and our games do not collect, your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise (GPS) location. Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to answer you.

## 3. Information collected when you play

**Gameplay analytics (GameAnalytics).** To understand how our games are played and to improve them, we use GameAnalytics. It collects technical and usage information such as device model and manufacturer, operating system version, game version, language, approximate country (derived from your IP address), randomly generated installation and session identifiers, session start and length, and in-game events such as levels started, completed or failed, ads watched and purchases made. Where permitted, this may include your device's advertising identifier.

**Advertising (AppLovin MAX and its ad partners).** Our games show ads through AppLovin MAX, which works with other advertising networks. To deliver ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud, these providers collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type and settings, approximate location, and your interactions with ads. **Where required by law, ads are only personalised with your consent** (see section 4).

**In-app purchases (Google Play).** Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation of what was purchased (for example, "Remove Ads") so we can unlock it.

## 4. Your choices and consent

- **Consent form.** If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the first time you open the game you will see a consent form (provided by Google's User Messaging Platform through AppLovin) where you can accept or refuse personalised advertising and related data processing.
- **Change your mind at any time** from the game's **Settings → Privacy Options**.
- **Advertising identifier.** On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Google or Privacy settings.
- **Ads without purchase.** Buying "Remove Ads" turns off interstitial (forced) ads. Optional ads you choose to watch for a reward remain available.

If you refuse personalised advertising, you may still see ads, but they will not be based on your personal data.

## 5. Service providers

These providers process information as described above. Some of them, especially advertising networks, act as independent controllers for their own purposes. Please read their policies:

| Provider | Purpose | Privacy policy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GameAnalytics | Gameplay analytics | https://gameanalytics.com/privacy |
| AppLovin (MAX) and its mediated ad networks | Advertising and consent management | https://www.applovin.com/privacy/ |
| Google (Google Play, AdMob / User Messaging Platform) | Distribution, purchases, consent form | https://policies.google.com/privacy |

The consent form lists the advertising partners that may process your data.

## 6. Legal bases (GDPR)

- **Providing the game and the items you buy:** performance of our contract with you.
- **Gameplay analytics:** our legitimate interest in understanding and improving our games. You can object at any time (see section 9).
- **Personalised advertising:** your consent, where required.
- **Non-personalised advertising, fraud prevention and security:** our legitimate interest in funding free games and keeping them safe.
- **Legal obligations**, for example tax records for purchases.

## 7. International transfers

Some providers process data outside the European Economic Area, for example in the United States. In those cases they rely on safeguards recognised by EU law, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 8. How long data is kept

We do not store your gameplay data ourselves. Our providers keep it for the periods described in their privacy policies; analytics data is kept in aggregated or pseudonymous form. We keep emails you send us only as long as needed to handle your request.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time without affecting processing already carried out. To exercise these rights, email **lluviastudios@outlook.com**. Because we do not know who you are, we may ask for information that lets our providers find the data linked to your device.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, https://www.aepd.es) or the authority in your country.

## 10. Children

Our games are suitable for all ages but are **not directed at children under 13**, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. In Spain, children under 14 may only consent to the processing of their personal data with the authorisation of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has provided personal data through our games, contact us and we will ask our providers to delete it.

## 11. Security

We rely on established providers that apply industry-standard security measures. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we choose providers that protect data appropriately.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add a game or a service provider. We will change the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the game.
